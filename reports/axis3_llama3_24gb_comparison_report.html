All five arms move together across rate — the story is rate, not arm. At every rate, the four adaptive/gated arms (adaptive-cap-only, kv-aware-cap-only, full) sit within a few percent of static-cap-hi on TTFT, E2E, and SLO. static-cap-lo is the one visibly different bar: its tight admission cap keeps KV occupancy lower (0.37–0.40 vs. 0.70–0.95 for everything else) but at the cost of higher TTFT/E2E at rate≥4, since requests queue longer behind the tighter cap.
