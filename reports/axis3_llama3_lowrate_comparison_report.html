Gated γ vs. fixed γ=4: the direct comparison

full (gated, reacts to load) against static-cap-hi (fixed γ=4, same admission cap) — isolates what the gate contributes on its own.

Read

γ climbs correctly with load — 1.99 at rate=0.5 up to 2.34 at rate=1.0 (the most decode-bound cell), then eases back to 1.66 as rate=2.0 tips toward admission-bound. But SLO attainment ties the fixed-γ=4 baseline at three of four rates; only rate=1.5 shows separation (0.378 vs 0.354), a 2.4-point gap on a single 60s/82-request run that is well inside plausible seed noise. adaptive-cap-only (γ pinned at the floor, 1) also matches static-cap-hi almost exactly at every rate — the strongest evidence here that speculation depth itself, gated or fixed, high or low, has very little leverage on this end-to-end metric in this regime. The gate is reading its input correctly; the input just isn't the lever that moves SLO attainment.
